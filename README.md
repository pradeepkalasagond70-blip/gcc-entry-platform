# GCC Entry Platform 

### India GCC Ecosystem Intelligence & Market Entry Platform

GCC Entry Platform is a product prototype designed to help global companies explore India's Global Capability Centre (GCC) ecosystem, compare potential locations, validate information, and plan their entry into the Indian market.

## Overview

India's GCC ecosystem includes multinational companies operating technology, finance, analytics, research, and business operations centres. However, the information required to establish a GCC is fragmented across government sources, company announcements, industry reports, real estate firms, talent providers, and professional service providers.

GCC Entry Platform aims to bring this information into one decision-support experience.

## Problem Statement

Companies entering India often face four key challenges:

- **Fragmented information:** GCC data is scattered across multiple sources.
- **Inconsistent figures:** Reports may publish different GCC counts and market estimates.
- **Limited transparency:** It can be difficult to validate service providers and their delivery track records.
- **Complex location decisions:** Companies must compare talent availability, office costs, incentives, infrastructure, and ecosystem maturity.

These challenges increase research effort and can lead to decisions based heavily on referrals rather than comparable evidence.

## Proposed Solution

The platform provides a unified workflow to discover GCC ecosystem information, compare cities, examine supporting evidence, explore potential service providers, and prepare for market entry.

The goal is not simply to create another directory. It is to make information easier to validate and use in real business decisions.

## Key Features

### 1. Investor Overview
An overview of the Indian GCC ecosystem and a starting point for defining market-entry requirements.

### 2. City Intelligence
Compare potential GCC locations using relevant ecosystem indicators and decision criteria.

### 3. GCC Directory
Explore companies and GCC-related information, with source references and verification status where available.

### 4. Evidence Explorer
Review supporting sources, publication dates, confidence levels, information gaps, and claims that require further validation.

### 5. Service Provider Discovery
Explore providers across legal and tax advisory, recruitment, real estate, payroll, and GCC setup services.

### 6. Entry Planning
Organise research priorities and identify the next steps required to evaluate an India entry.

## Target Users

**Primary users**
- Global companies evaluating India for their first GCC
- Corporate strategy and GCC expansion teams

**Potential future users**
- GCC service providers
- Real estate and talent ecosystem participants
- Investors, researchers, and market analysts

## Product Differentiation

The platform is designed around evidence-backed decision-making rather than directory listings alone.

Its core principles are:

- Trace important claims to their sources.
- Distinguish verified information from reported claims and assumptions.
- Make uncertainty and conflicting figures visible.
- Help users compare options against their specific requirements.
- Avoid presenting illustrative estimates as established facts.

## Prototype Scope

This repository contains an early-stage prototype intended to demonstrate the product concept and user workflow.

- Some information is sourced from published reports and company announcements.
- Some city comparisons or scores may use illustrative assumptions.
- The prototype is not a complete, independently verified census of India's GCCs.
- Paid API subscriptions and production-grade live data integrations are not included.
- Information should be independently verified before being used for investment or business decisions.

## Future Roadmap

1. Expand the source-backed GCC company and centre database.
2. Integrate suitable public and licensed data sources.
3. Add structured city-level information on talent, office costs, infrastructure, and state incentives.
4. Develop transparent service-provider evaluation and verification workflows.
5. Introduce automated data freshness checks and conflict detection.
6. Validate the product with companies planning to establish or expand a GCC in India.

## Technology Approach

The prototype demonstrates a product workflow for ecosystem discovery, evidence validation, city comparison, and entry planning.

A future production implementation could combine structured data ingestion, source provenance, data normalisation, validation workflows, and a searchable intelligence layer.
